## Get photos to devices
Getting photos to the creators devices is the most complicated part of making a flatgame - even if you have tested it out!

--- task ---
If this is slow, you can start making the flatgame using placeholder images in the starter code. This will help creators to get going while you distribute photos. 

They can then go back and add the photos into the project.
--- /task ---


--- task ---
It could help to make a list of everyone in the club and check off when they have their photos on their device.
--- /task ---
