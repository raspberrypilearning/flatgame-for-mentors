## Choosing a theme

The first part of the session is based on hand-on drawing and making based on a theme.

### Helping creators choose a theme
The first few mins of the session in based around choosing a theme - this helps to give focus to the artwork. There are some suggestions for promts below, but anything that is not offensive or harms people is acceptible to use. 

--- task ---
Help creators decide on a theme based on these promts:

- a place they like to visit
- something they like to do
- your favourite game

Or, make a theme based on the club itself. 👀 Ask creators to look around the room you are in, what do they notice, what is interesting or different about it? Do they have a memory from the club they could start with. Is there a project they loved to create?
--- /task ---
