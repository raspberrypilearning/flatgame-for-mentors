##  Making the flatgame
The rest of the project uses Scratch to make the game. 

There is a starter project, and 3 example projects that creators can see for inspiration. 

Flatgames are not supposed to be neat. They have a cut-out, hand-crafted feel. Encourage young people to make a mess!

If you have time at the end of the session ask creators if they will share thier project. This can be very informal, and they can move around the room and try out each other's flatgames.
